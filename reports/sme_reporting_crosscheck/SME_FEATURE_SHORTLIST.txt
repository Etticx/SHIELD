REPORTING STRUCTURE
Balance sheet: assets, liabilities, equity and relevant current/non-current breakdowns.
Profit/loss: revenue, costs and profit; scope and subtotals vary.
Equity statement/notes: share capital, retained earnings/accumulated losses and movements.
Cash-flow statement: operating, investing and financing flows; net CFO is not simply cash generated from operations.
Notes: borrowing, interest/finance costs, depreciation, shares and other material details.
IFRS XHTML supplied by user:173 distinct tagged concepts. Example includes R&D and shares, but not every SME must have every example line.
