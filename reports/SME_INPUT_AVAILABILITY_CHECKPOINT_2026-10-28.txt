IMPORTANT SCOPE CHANGE
Earlier classification allowed recovery only from other top-20 ratios. This checkpoint permits calculations from the SME's financial statements and notes. More features therefore become feasible in principle.
No input is guaranteed for every SME. The working scope is an incorporated SME that supplies a complete annual financial statement set, notes, and relevant prior-year comparatives. Sole proprietors and firms with insufficient history need separate treatment.

1. RAW INFORMATION TO LOOK FOR
Balance sheet: total assets, total liabilities, total equity; opening/prior-year balances where needed.
Equity statement/notes: retained earnings or accumulated losses; share capital and share details where applicable.
Profit/loss statement: revenue, profit before tax, profit after tax, income-tax expense.
Statement/notes, when separately disclosed: interest expense, other finance costs, short/long-term borrowings, depreciation/amortisation, disposal gains/losses, continuing-operation profit and share movements.
Availability must be checked on the actual documents; notes and accountant schedules may be needed. Audit exemption does not itself prove financial records are unavailable.
The 20 SHIELD features are ratios or specialised measures, not 20 guaranteed line items printed in SME accounts.

2. EIGHT FIRST-PRIORITY CALCULATOR CANDIDATES
The following are simplified accounting concepts, NOT verified exact historical donor formulas or model-ready values. Confirm periods, ending versus average balances, numerator scope, ownership scope, denominator treatment, and original transformation before implementation.
- Net profit before tax/Paid-in capital: pretax profit relative to contributed capital. Exact capital components require confirmation; share capital is not total equity.
- Net Income to Stockholder's Equity: net income relative to the appropriate shareholders' equity balance.
- Net Income to Total Assets: net income relative to the appropriate asset balance.
- Retained Earnings to Total Assets: retained earnings relative to assets.
- Debt ratio %: total liabilities relative to assets; confirm fraction versus percent.
- Net worth/Assets: appropriate equity relative to assets.
- Equity to Liability: appropriate equity relative to liabilities.
- Liability to Equity: liabilities relative to appropriate equity; check sign and absolute-value conventions.
These calculations use the company's real recorded amounts, not another company's typical ratio.

3. TWELVE INPUTS NOT CONFIRMED AVAILABLE/REPRODUCIBLE
These are a provisional review list, not proof they are impossible for every SME or an instruction to delete all twelve.
A. History/share-detail needs:
- Persistent EPS in the Last Four Seasons: specialised four-quarter recurring-earnings/share information; do not substitute ordinary annual EPS automatically.
- Per Share Net profit before tax: needs the matching share-count convention and pretax earnings.
- Net Value Per Share (A): exact A convention and applicable share/equity adjustments needed.
- Net Value Per Share (B): exact B convention and applicable share/equity adjustments needed; do not copy A.
B. Financial detail, formula, or historical mapping needs:
- Borrowing dependency: borrowing breakdown and correct equity definition needed; total liabilities are not all borrowing.
- ROA(A) before interest and % after tax: correct earnings/tax/interest adjustments and asset period needed.
- ROA(B) before interest and depreciation after tax: adjusted earnings, tax, finance costs, depreciation/amortisation and asset period needed.
- ROA(C) before interest and depreciation before interest: appropriate recurring earnings, finance costs, depreciation/amortisation and asset period needed.
- Interest Expense Ratio: exact numerator and denominator unresolved for donor column; do not assume a familiar formula.
- Interest Coverage Ratio (Interest expense to EBIT): direction conflict with usual coverage name; confirm donor convention.
- Continuous interest rate (after tax): likely recurring-profit margin concept, not bank loan interest rate; historical mapping must be confirmed.
- Degree of Financial Leverage (DFL): may be calculable with operating profit and interest, but exact convention and undefined-value handling need confirmation.

TEJ DOCUMENTATION FINDINGS
Current TEJ documentation explains recurring earnings, differing ROA adjustments, share-value variants, borrowing dependence and leverage. These support conditional calculation feasibility, not automatic deletion. Four-quarter EPS includes recurring-profit and share adjustments. Annual accounts may supply some ingredients without supplying every specialised adjustment. Current vendor definitions are not proof of the UCI 1999-2009 field mapping. An interest-coverage label ambiguity remains. Current Taiwan tax/share conventions must not be blindly applied to Malaysian SMEs.

MODEL COMPATIBILITY
Financial statement availability, correct ratio definition, and model-compatible transformation are three separate checks. The saved 20-feature model uses transformed values. We have not established a verified original donor transformation from raw RM amounts/ratios into its input scale. Do not min-max normalise each SME's 20 fields together, infer donor bounds from normalised training columns, or feed raw calculated ratios as if they were already matching model inputs.

3. CANDIDATES FOR FUTURE REMOVAL EXPERIMENTS
Priority scenario: four-quarter recurring EPS unavailable.
Second scenario: share-related history/details unavailable (four fields above).
Conditional individual/group scenarios: any remaining specialised fields whose definitions or actual records cannot be obtained after document review.
Do not label all twelve permanently unavailable. Do not delete the eight calculator candidates merely because the ratio is not printed in the financial statement.
No imputation used in this proposed path. Retraining/evaluation without required unavailable fields is a later stage; no such training was performed for this research checkpoint.
