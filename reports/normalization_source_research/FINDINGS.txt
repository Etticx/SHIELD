PRIMARY PAPER VS RELEASE
Liang et al. (2016), https://isslab.csie.ncu.edu.tw/download/publications/1.pdf
Section 3.1 explicitly uses per-feature min-max normalisation, but discusses
239 bankrupt + 239 nonbankrupt cases with 95 FRs and 95 governance indicators.
That is not the same stated sample as the 6,819-row/95-predictor UCI release.
Neither the original raw bounds nor a verified link between that particular
478-case fitted scaler and the published UCI values has been recovered.

https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/572/taiwanese+bankruptcy+prediction
Cached official ZIP has data.csv only, as previously checked. The entire local
Excel dataset matches the official release numerically after column alignment.
The unknown representation is present in the UCI release; it was not created
by SHIELD's seven-feature pipeline.

ADDITIONAL DIRECT NUMERIC CHECK
Of all 95 official predictors, 24 contain values outside [0,1]. Example:
Operating Expense Rate reaches 9,990,000,000; R&D expense rate reaches
9,980,000,000. All seven SHIELD features individually have min 0 and max 1.
Therefore do not repeat the blanket claim that the entire public dataset is a
clean 0-1 min-max table. This does not prove why the large values occur; possible
mixed representation/data anomalies remain hypotheses.
Measured full nonunit list: released_value_ranges.json.

OTHER PAPERS DO NOT AUTOMATICALLY SOLVE THIS
Many later studies describe fitting StandardScaler, MinMaxScaler or power
transforms to the downloaded file. These are additional transformations of the
released values, not necessarily conversion from real statement ratios. A later
paper using the word 'raw' often means 'as downloaded', not original TEJ amounts.
No recovered bounds were found in the reviewed public paper/repository sources.
Do not assume a saved scaler from another notebook bridges the missing raw step.

CONCLUSION
The seven columns cannot currently be treated as unchanged accounting ratios.
Min-max scaling is documented for a related study, but the exact release-level
transformation, original bounds and preprocessing history remain unverified.
This investigation found independent public reports of the same issue, plus
an explicit uploader correction of column metadata; it did not recover the
missing original parameters. Do not claim the values are irrecoverably lost,
or that arbitrary bounds have been established by synthetic sanity checks.
No current application/model changes were made in this research run.
