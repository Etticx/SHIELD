Direct checks, 5year.arff, first data row:
X1=0.088238; X2=0.55472; X6=0.34204; X8=0.57752; X10=0.32036.
X10/X2 ~= 0.57752, consistent with X8. Values across the file include negative
numbers and numbers above 1, so this is not blanket 0-1 min-max output.
This observation alone does not prove exact correspondence to every raw
financial-statement convention.

Caution: among 5,891 rows with finite X2/X8/X10 and nonzero X2,
5,853 satisfy abs(X8-X10/X2)<0.001, but only 3,891 satisfy
abs(X2+X10-1)<0.001. This discrepancy needs investigation; do not claim full
accounting consistency. Selected columns have missing data: X1/X2/X6/X10 each
have 3 missing entries; X8 has 18 in this file. Missing marked with '?'.
Extreme and negative liabilities ratios also warrant a definition/quality review.
